## Description (4000 chars max)

**Roofax is your home's permanent digital record — built and maintained by the contractors who work on it.**

Every roof replaced. Every HVAC serviced. Every fence installed. Every upgrade made. All documented with real photos, warranties, and receipts — organized in one place that belongs to you forever.

**HOW IT WORKS**

When a contractor completes work on your home, they push the record directly to Roofax. You get:
• Before, during, and after photos
• Warranty details and expiration dates
• Contractor info and license numbers
• Total cost and payment records
• A permanent, timestamped history

No more digging through email for invoices. No more lost warranty cards. No more "who did that work?" before a home sale.

**WHY HOMEOWNERS LOVE IT**

🏠 **Own your home's story**
Your records belong to you — not the contractor, not an app subscription. Even if you switch contractors or sell your home, your records stay with you.

📸 **Real photos, not paperwork**
Contractors upload job photos directly from the field. You see exactly what was done and when.

🔒 **Your privacy, protected**
Contractors can add to your record but can't see other trades' work unless you grant access. You control who sees what.

💰 **Increase your home's resale value**
Documented maintenance history is a proven selling point. Show buyers exactly what's been done and when — with verified photos and warranties.

🤝 **Trusted contractors only**
Every contractor in your record is verified and licensed. No anonymous work, no missing info.

**FREE FOR HOMEOWNERS — ALWAYS**

Roofax is 100% free for homeowners. No subscription. No hidden fees. No data selling. Contractors pay to use the platform — your records are always yours at no cost.

**GET STARTED IN SECONDS**
1. Create your free account
2. Enter your property address
3. Your contractors push records automatically — or you can add past work manually

Whether you're a new homeowner building your first file or a long-time owner finally getting organized, Roofax gives your home the documentation it deserves.
